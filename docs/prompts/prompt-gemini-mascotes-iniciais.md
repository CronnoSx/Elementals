# Prompts para o Gemini: mascotes dos 6 biomas iniciais

## Como usar

1. Em todo pedido, **anexe 1 ou 2 personagens do jogo** (por exemplo `characters/char_03.png` e `char_10.png`). Assim o Gemini copia o traço, as cores e o clima aconchegante dos heróis.
2. Gere **um mascote por vez**: cole o **bloco de estilo** e, logo abaixo, o bloco do mascote.
3. Quando gostar de um mascote, anexe ele também nos próximos pedidos, para os 6 saírem com cara de mesma família.
4. Depois que os 6 estiverem prontos, use o **prompt de folha de modelo** (no final) com cada um, para ter frente, lado, costas e expressões.

**Cuidado com cópia:** criaturas elementais lembram muito Pokémon e Digimon. Por isso cada mascote tem um formato pouco comum, e o estilo pede um design original. Se algum resultado lembrar um monstrinho famoso, descarte e gere outra vez.

Os nomes são provisórios.

---

## Bloco de estilo (cole sempre primeiro)

```
Style: match the attached hero characters exactly: soft 2D painterly storybook illustration, cozy and warm, gentle outlines, subtle texture, muted natural colors with one vivid element accent, same lighting and rendering quality. Cute but not babyish, slightly chubby proportions, big expressive eyes, readable silhouette. Original creature design, must NOT resemble Pokémon, Digimon or any existing franchise character.

Format: single full-body creature, standing in a friendly pose, 3/4 view, centered, plain light background, no text, no logo, no frame. Size relation: the creature is about knee-to-waist height next to the hero.
```

Creature: a small round newt-like creature whose body looks like it is made of clear blue water, with a darker blue core visible inside like a pebble. Its head frills are flowing ribbons of water that curl at the tips, and tiny bubbles float around it. Short stubby legs, a wide happy smile, a curious, playful personality. ## 2. Fogo: "Fornalinho" (v2, mais fogo e mais estressado)

Para **editar a imagem já gerada** (anexe ela):

```
Keep this exact creature design (same clay kiln body, shape, proportions, chimney and painterly style), but change:
1. The opening on its belly now holds a real, living fire: bright tall flames in yellow, orange and red that lick out of the opening and over the edge, casting a warm orange glow on its body and on the ground. No heart shape.
2. Small flames also burst from the top of the chimney, with a much bigger, thicker plume of dark gray smoke billowing upward, plus lots of sparks and embers flying around.
3. Glowing orange cracks on its body, like hot coals showing through the clay.
4. Personality: hot-tempered and stressed. Furrowed angry eyebrows, gritted teeth or a grumpy pout, both fists clenched, a small puff of steam from the side of its head, stomping pose. Still cute, not scary.
Only one single image of the creature, large, 1024x1024, plain light background, no text.
```

## Folha de modelo (depois de escolher cada mascote)

Anexe a imagem escolhida do mascote e cole:

```
Using the attached creature as the exact design (same shape, colors, proportions and details, do not change anything), create a character model sheet on a plain light background: front view, side view, back view, and four small facial expressions (happy, angry/attacking, scared, celebrating). Same painterly storybook style. Clean layout, evenly spaced, no text labels.
```

Essa folha serve para depois criarmos as animações e as reações do mascote no jogo, como na hora de fazer cadeia, de levar cristal ou de vencer.

Anexe a primeira versão (fofa) e a segunda (brava) e cole:
